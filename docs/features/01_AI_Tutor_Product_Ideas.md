# AI Tutor Bangladesh — Product Ideas

## Product Opportunity

A Bangladesh-focused AI learning platform designed around Bangla-first interaction, NCTB/SSC curriculum, local exam patterns, and affordable mobile-first learning.

## Initial High-Potential Ideas

- Bangla-first personal AI tutor
- Photo-based question solver
- Adaptive quizzes
- Personalized study plans
- Weak-topic detection
- AI textbook assistant
- Exam preparation mode
- English tutor
- Voice tutor
- Parent dashboard
- Teacher dashboard

## Recommended Initial Market

Focus first on Class 9–10 / SSC students, especially the Science group, instead of attempting to cover every education level immediately.

## Initial Subjects

- Mathematics
- Physics
- Chemistry
- Biology
- ICT
- English

## Product Differentiator

Position the product as:

> A personal AI tutor that understands the Bangladesh curriculum.

The goal is not to create another generic AI chatbot. The core advantage should come from curriculum-grounded answers, Bangla/Banglish support, student-specific learning history, and adaptive practice.

## Monetization Ideas

### Free

- Limited AI questions/day
- Basic quizzes
- Basic study plan
- Limited photo questions

### Premium

- Higher AI usage
- Photo problem solving
- Personalized learning
- Advanced quizzes
- Exam preparation
- Progress analytics

### Family Plan

Potentially around ৳499–699/month for multiple children.

### B2B

School/coaching subscriptions priced per student.
